# Open Questions and Ambiguities (arXiv 2611.34911v1)

English | [日本語](open-questions.ja.md)

The ambiguities from the three analysis documents are collected in one table with fresh IDs. Line numbers `l.NNN` refer to `.cache/paper/2611.34911v1.txt`.

- Abbreviations for the source documents: **01** = `01_preliminaries_primitives.md` (A1–A19 in §4 and others), **02** = `02_ordered_hierarchy_local.md` (A1–A18 in §5 and others), **03** = `03_pivots_aux_outer.md` (1–9 in §5 and others), **TH** = `thresholds.md`. "New" marks points found during this consolidation.
- Status:
  - **Decided by interpretation**: the reading is unique, or the choice does not change the result. Implement the provisional interpretation and confirm it with tests.
  - **Needs agreement**: requires agreement among several owners (or the lead). Once agreed, record the decision and date here, and write an ADR if needed.
  - **Open**: the paper does not determine the reading, and its impact has not been assessed.
- Points where documents overlap or disagree are merged into one row, and both views are recorded. We never silently pick one.
- For the corresponding deviations (D-xx), see `docs/PAPER_MAPPING.md` §3.
